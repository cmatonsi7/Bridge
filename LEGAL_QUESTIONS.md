# Legal questions for the client's legal adviser

A running list, kept as we build, to be reviewed together at the end. These are questions, not answers:
the developers are not lawyers. Each item says where it arises in the product.

| # | Question | Where it arises |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Child data shared on application.** An organisation receives a child's name, age, sport and profile when they apply. What consent wording must the parent give, and when? | Applications (Phase 3b) |
| 2 | **Adult applicants' contact details.** Organisations receive an adult applicant's email and phone number. Is notice at the point of applying enough? | Applications (Phase 3b) |
| 3 | **Coaches listed without signing up.** What permission is needed to list a coach we added from a list, and how must it be recorded? | Onboarding step C |
| 4 | **Invitation emails to coaches.** Do they count as unsolicited electronic marketing under POPIA? | Onboarding step C |
| 5 | **Coaches working with children.** Should police clearance or a sexual-offenders-register check be required? What may we store (we propose only "checked by staff on [date]")? | Connections (Phase 3c) |
| 6 | **Sponsorship.** Is publishing a child's financial need, even without their name or photo, acceptable? May a parent's contact details be shared with a sponsor once the parent accepts? | Sponsorship (Phase 3d) |
| 7 | **Age rules.** Is 13 the right minimum for self-registration? Should anyone under 18 be limited to athlete accounts? | Sign-up (Phase 1) |
| 8 | **Data held abroad.** The database is in Johannesburg but web hosting is in Frankfurt. Does this count as a cross-border transfer needing a safeguard? | Hosting |
| 9 | **Retention and deletion.** How long do we keep applications, audit logs and claim records, and how do people request erasure? | All phases |
| 10 | **Staff access.** What controls and records must exist when staff can view personal information? | Admin dashboard |
| 11 | **Information Officer.** Who is the client's Information Officer, and are they registered with the Information Regulator? | Launch |
| 12 | **The documents themselves.** Privacy and POPIA notice, Terms of Use, and Safety & Child Protection. They are listed in the site footer as plain text until they exist. | Launch |
| 13 | **Subscriptions.** The Organisation plan has a 30-day opt-in trial with no automatic billing. What consumer-protection requirements apply to the paid plans? | Payments (Phase 4) |
| 14 | **Imported school information.** If we publish a school's details from a public list, what are our duties around accuracy and removal on request? | Onboarding step A |
| 15 | **Claim codes.** Staff give a school a code that lets its representative take over the record. Is a code sufficient proof of authority, or should the client verify the representative some other way before the school is shown as verified? | Onboarding step A |
| 16 | **The Verified badge.** Staff verify an organisation after their own checks, and families rely on the badge. What must "verified" mean in the client's terms, what checks must staff make and record, and what responsibility does the client take on by displaying it? Staff also keep a private internal note on each decision: how long may it be kept, and may the organisation request to see it? | Verification (Phase 4) |
